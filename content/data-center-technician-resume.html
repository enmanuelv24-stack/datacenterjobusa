Data center technician résumé: what gets you the call

Recruiters for technician roles skim a résumé in seconds. They look for hands-on work with hardware, cabling or power, safety, shift availability and whether you can start soon. Make those easy to find.

The five things to put near the top

  1. Location and willingness to travel or relocate. Many jobs are on new campuses in small towns; say if you can go.
  2. Shift availability. "Available for nights, weekends and 12-hour rotations" answers the recruiter's first question.
  3. Hands-on skills, in their words. Rack and stack, structured cabling, fiber termination and testing, server hardware swaps, cable management, labeling, UPS, switchgear, generators, BMS.
  4. Safety. OSHA 10/30, NFPA 70E, LOTO, lift or forklift training.
  5. Certifications you actually hold (see which ones pay off).

Write bullets with numbers

"Installed servers" says little. "Racked, cabled and labeled 40+ servers per shift on a hyperscale build, zero rework on QA walk" says a lot. Count racks, cables, tickets, sites, hours, uptime, people trained.

Coming from another field?

  • Electricians and HVAC techs: lead with your trade license and the equipment you've serviced; critical facilities roles post some of the highest pay ranges we track.
  • Help desk / IT support: hardware troubleshooting, imaging, ticketing and documentation transfer directly.
  • Military: translate your rate or MOS into plain tasks (power generation, electronics maintenance, comms) and mention any clearance.
  • Low-voltage, fiber and cabling crews: list the cable types, testers (OTDR, power meter, certifier) and how much you terminated.

A one-page example

Alex Rivera · Abilene, TX · open to travel and 12-hour rotations · (555) 555-0100 · alex@example.com

Summary. Low-voltage technician with 3 years of structured cabling and fiber work on commercial and data center builds. OSHA 10, CompTIA A+. Available nights and weekends; can start in two weeks.

Experience. Cabling Technician, ABC Low Voltage (2023–present)

  • Pulled, terminated and tested Cat6A and single-mode fiber on a 3-building data center build; passed QA on first walk.
  • Labeled and documented 2,000+ runs to the client's standard; trained 4 new helpers.
  • Used fiber inspection scopes, power meters and certifiers; logged results for turnover.

Skills. Rack and stack · fiber termination and testing · cable management · server hardware swaps · ticketing · ladder and lift safety

Certifications. OSHA 10 · CompTIA A+

The example name, numbers and contact details are placeholders; use your own real experience.

What to leave off

  • Site names, badge details or anything a client considers confidential.
  • Photos, age, and long lists of unrelated software.
  • More than two pages. One page is best for most technician roles.
